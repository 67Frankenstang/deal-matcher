Deal Matcher

Weekly ads

Any store works: the app reads store names from the chain column of deals.json and builds the store list, colours, filters and shop sections from that. Add a row for Aldi or Walmart and it just appears. The file is the 16-column schema (an object with schema, ad_weeks, rows) or a plain array of row objects.

Get the raw data

Every deal currently loaded, in the 16-column schema. The CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets; the JSON is the file the app reads.

My stores

Untick a store and it disappears from your list, shop totals and best-price picks on this device. Deals tab still shows everything.

Shopping list

Paste a list from another app (one item per line, or America's Test Kitchen's category-grouped copy) and add every line at once.

How prices are labeled

Solid yellow tag = a price printed in the ad. ≤ amber tag = a ceiling worked out from "save up to" or "regular retail up to" wording; the shelf price may be lower. Shop totals keep these apart as firm vs. estimated.

DC means you need to clip the digital coupon in the store app first.
